# ontology-detach

Enforces exit conditions on every binding.

Slash command: `/ontology-detach`

Hardcoding is a lie — it always breaks over time. De-existence (탈존재화) means designing without clinging to specific values, states, or assumptions.

See [skill file](https://github.com/HHSsub/ontology-driven-design/blob/main/skills/ontology-detach/SKILL.md) for full details.
